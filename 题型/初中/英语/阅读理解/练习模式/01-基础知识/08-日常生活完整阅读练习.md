## 题目

### 阅读材料

A day in the emperor’s life

The emperor (皇帝) gets up at 5 a.m. More than 20 people help him wash his face, brush his teeth, comb his hair and put on his clothes.

After getting up, the emperor goes to hear morning reports. At about 7 a.m., he does some exercise, such as riding a horse or learning kung fu. One hour later, he has breakfast. There are more than 20 dishes on the table, including chicken, duck, vegetables, soup and porridge (粥). In the morning, the emperor studies and works. He reads books and has classes. He also reads zouzhe and writes his replies.

At 2 p.m., it’s lunch time! Like breakfast, the emperor has lots of delicious food. After lunch, he relaxes for a while. He can take a walk in the garden, write poems, or watch Chinese operas. He can also play with his pets, such as parrots.

Many people think that being an emperor is great, but he always stays up (熬夜) late reading zouzhe. Sometimes he goes to bed at 2 a.m.!

### 题目

1.（原题1）When does the emperor have breakfast?

- A: 6 a.m.
- B: 8 a.m.
- C: 7 a.m.
- D: 5 a.m.

**答案（维护信息）：** B  
**考点：** 查找时间并结合前后句计算。  
**解析：** 皇帝大约七点锻炼，一个小时后吃早饭，因此是上午八点。

2.（原题2）What’s the meaning of “relax for a while” in the third paragraph?

- A: have a break
- B: have some tea
- C: have some practice
- D: have a talk

**答案（维护信息）：** A  
**考点：** 根据并列活动理解短语。  
**解析：** 散步、写诗、看戏和与宠物玩都属于休息放松的活动；have a break 意为休息一会儿。

3.（原题3）What does NOT the emperor do in a day?

- A: Read articles by officials (官员).
- B: Watch Chinese operas.
- C: Have afternoon tea.
- D: Ride a horse.

**答案（维护信息）：** C  
**考点：** 核对选项是否有原文依据。  
**解析：** 文中提到读奏折、看戏和骑马，没有提到喝下午茶。

4.（原题4）Which is TRUE about the emperor’s life?

- A: He is good at kung fu.
- B: He washes and changes clothes by himself.
- C: He raises crickets and parrots as pets.
- D: He has lunch at 2 p.m.

**答案（维护信息）：** D  
**考点：** 核对具体时间信息。  
**解析：** 第三段明确说下午两点是午餐时间。文章只说他有鹦鹉等宠物，没有说养蟋蟀；也没有说他擅长功夫或自己穿衣。

5.（原题5）What can we infer from the last paragraph?

- A: It is wonderful to be an emperor.
- B: The emperor enjoys his life.
- C: Being a good emperor is not easy.
- D: Staying up late is his hobby.

**答案（维护信息）：** C  
**考点：** 从夜间工作和很晚休息推断人物处境。  
**解析：** 皇帝夜里还要读奏折，有时凌晨两点才睡；这说明皇帝仍承担繁重事务，当好皇帝并不容易。

# 02 细节题基本流程

## 做前说明

```
做细节题按三步走：

1. 划关键词：从题干里挑一个少见、有特征、文中不容易重复出现的词。
2. 定位：带着这个词去文章里找它出现的地方（它可能换了说法）。
3. 附近找答案：读定位句和它前后必要的部分，用自己的话说出原文的意思，再对选项。

找到关键词不等于找到答案，一定要把定位处读清楚。
```

## 题目

### 阅读材料

A wounded (受伤的) great white pelican (鹈鹕) chose a good place to land. A storm made it leave its group. Visitors at the resort (度假胜地) thought the pelican was an adult because it was big. But the workers there soon found out that the pelican was only about three months old. They named it Big Bird and took good care of it to make it healthy again.

The workers knew Big Bird needed lots of food to reach its full adult size. In the wild, a group of pelicans work together to catch fish. But Big Bird didn’t have a group, so the workers fed it fish. Eating fish, Big Bird got bigger.

Everyone at the resort hoped that Big Bird would fly away and go back to its group when it got healthy. But Big Bird needed to learn how to fly! The workers tried to teach it.

After more practice, Big Bird showed its growing number of fans a successful flight (飞行). Just as it was getting dark, Big Bird landed at the same place where it took off. “We’re so proud of it. It can fly!” Big Bird’s trainers said.

No one knows how long Big Bird will stay at the resort. Maybe one day it will fly away, find a group of pelicans to be with, and never come back to the place where it learned to fly. But before that happens, the workers and visitors are happy to be with it.

### 题目

原题51：What caused Big Bird to leave its group?

A. The terrible weather.  
B. Trying to find food.  
C. Being hurt by people.  
D. Getting lost on long trips.

**答案（维护信息）：** A  
**考点：** 选关键词、定位并读懂附近证据。  
**解析：** 题干中的 Big Bird 和 leave its group 可用于定位到第一段。原文写道 “A storm made it leave its group.”，storm 对应 terrible weather。先找到相关句，再核对原因与选项意思。

**来源与核验：** 广东省深圳市福田区红岭实验学校（上沙）2025—2026学年八年级上学期开学英语试题，阅读理解原题第51题。原文、题干、选项与答案经核对。与01/03关复用同篇材料，目标不同。

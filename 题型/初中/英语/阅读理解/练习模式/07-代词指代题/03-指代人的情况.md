# 03 指代人的情况

## 做前说明

```
代词指人时，要看两件事：

- 单复数对不对（he / she 是单数，they / them 是复数或一群人）；
- 人物信息对不对（这句说的行为、身份，能不能安在这个人身上）。

单复数不匹配的，先排除。
```

## 题目

### 阅读材料

Ms. Spira, a music teacher, was the host of the talent show. She said, “There is only one place left. The two people left have to compete (竞争) for it.” Tameka and Kai were worried and looked at each other across the room. Tameka, a talented dancer, hoped to show her skills (技巧) on the show, while Kai, a talented pianist, dreamed of becoming a great musician and wanted to show himself, too.

Kai moved near Tameka. “I know you’re a wonderful dancer, and you know I do well in playing the piano. Too bad! We have to compete for the only one place,” he said.

“Oh, one of us will be sad,” Tameka answered.

Kai asked, “What music are you dancing to?”

Tameka answered that she planned to dance to A Comme Amour. Kai planned to play a classical waltz (古典华尔兹) by Frederic Chopin. But he also knew A Comme Amour and could play it, so he told something to Tameka, and Tameka smiled.

Then Ms. Spira turned to Tameka and Kai. She asked, “Who’s next?”

“Both of us—we decided to become a team!” **they** answered.

### 题目

In the last paragraph, who does “they” refer to?

A. Ms. Spira and Tameka.  
B. Tameka and Kai.  
C. Kai and Ms. Spira.  
D. Tameka and the other students.

**答案（维护信息）：** B  
**考点：** 根据复数形式和相邻事件匹配人物。  
**解析：** Ms. Spira 刚问 Tameka 和 Kai 谁下一个，两人回答 “Both of us”，随后说决定组成团队；因此 they 指 Tameka and Kai。

**来源与核验：** 广东省潮州市高级实验学校2024—2025学年八年级上学期11月期中英语试题，阅读理解原题第56—60题。由原文末段改编指代问法，人物与答案按对话上下文核对。

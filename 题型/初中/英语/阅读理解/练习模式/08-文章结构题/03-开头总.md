# 03 开头总

## 做前说明

```
读第一段（或开头几句），问：它有没有把文章要讲的对象或观点摆出来？

如果有，开头就是"总"；如果第一段直接进入具体的事或例子，那就是直接"分"，不必硬找总起句。
```

## 题目

### 阅读材料

Shenzhen, already famous for its clean and smart public restrooms, is making headlines again. It has built special “pet toilets” in many communities and shopping malls, which are designed to solve the problem of pet waste.

The Coast City shopping mall in Nanshan District has built a dedicated restroom for cats and dogs. This pet restroom is located next to regular restrooms and is provided with pet pee pads, deodorizers (除臭剂), and other items. On the walls, there are instructions on the proper ways to deal with pet waste. The overall environment is clean.

Many communities and shopping areas also provide pet waste cleaning tools for pet owners. In One Mall in Yantian District, there are sites for pet owners to pick up doggy waste bags. The Upperhills shopping mall in Futian District also provides trash bins with doggy waste bags and hand-wiping paper for cleaning up pet waste.

“Some dogs like chihuahuas (吉娃娃) and shih tzus (西施犬) can manage to go to the bathroom at home, but for larger dogs, outdoor toileting is a basic necessity,” said a woman who owns a small Scottish terrier (苏格兰梗犬) named AD. “The pet toilets meet the physiological (生理的) needs of pets while also creating a cleaner, more comfortable public space for those who do not own pets.”

Shenzhen has long been a pioneer in creating a pet-friendly environment. As early as 2020, the city became the first in China to ban the consumption of cats and dogs. Today, over 50 malls have pet-friendly facilities, with more on the way.

### 题目

What is the main role of Paragraph 1 in the passage?

A. It introduces Shenzhen’s pet toilets and the problem they aim to solve.  
B. It tells the detailed story of one pet owner.  
C. It gives the final result of a survey about pets.  
D. It lists every rule for using a pet restroom.

**答案（维护信息）：** A  
**考点：** 判断开头段是否统领后续内容。  
**解析：** 第一段提出深圳建设宠物厕所及其目的；第二、三段继续列设施实例，第四段说明对宠物和市民的意义，第五段补充城市层面的宠物友好举措。A 说明了开头段如何引出后文。

**来源与核验：** 2025年广东省深圳市罗湖外国语学校中考三模英语试题，阅读理解原题第321—325题。依据完整文章改编开头功能题；将原文中与复数 pet toilets 不一致的 which is designed 改为 which are designed，并在本关仅作单复数订正。与08/01、08/02关复用同篇材料，重复材料不作未见迁移证据。
**术语订正：** 将 shih tzu 和 Scottish terrier 的中文注释分别订正为“西施犬”和“苏格兰梗犬”。

# 06 总可以出现多处

## 做前说明

```
"总"不一定只有一处，常见变体有：

- 总—分—总：开头提出，中间展开，结尾收束；
- 总—分—总—总：结尾连用两段总结；
- 总—总—分—总：开头两段都在提中心，中间才展开。

所以判断结构时，把每一段的功能都看一眼，不要只找"一个开头、一个结尾"。
```

## 题目

### 阅读材料

Do animals communicate with each other? Some scientists did some research and, to their surprise, they learned that all animals communicate. They don’t use language, but they do send messages to each other. They learned that animals communicate with each other in three main ways: through sounds, smells, and body language.

The alarm call is one way that sounds are used for communication. When an enemy is near, animals send out a warning sound. Those who hear it run to safety, hide, or get together to fight the enemy. Dogs bark (吠叫) to show other animals how they’re feeling. Whales are well known for their ability to communicate from far away with special sounds. Humans don’t understand the meaning of these sounds, but in their own social groups, whales understand what they’re saying to each other.

Smell is another popular method of animal communication. Some organisms, such as amoebas (变形虫), give out a chemical to tell other amoebas it’s going to have babies. Some animals send out a strong smell to warn other animals to stay away from them. For example, skunks (北美臭鼬) give off an odour when they feel they are in danger.

There are many examples of animals that use body language to communicate. Chimpanzees (黑猩猩) touch hands when they meet. When angry, they stick out their tongues (舌头). Think of a happy dog moving its tail from side to side. Even humans understand this form of dog body language.

Humans have developed their own system to communicate with each other, using words and sentences. But if you watch other animals carefully for a while, you quickly come to understand that they also have ways of getting their messages across.

### 题目

Which choice best describes the roles of Paragraphs 1 and 5?

A. Paragraph 1 introduces the three main ways animals communicate; Paragraphs 2–4 explain them; Paragraph 5 returns to the main idea and closes the passage.  
B. Paragraph 1 gives one animal example; Paragraphs 2–5 list four unrelated animal stories.  
C. Paragraph 1 states the conclusion; Paragraph 5 begins a new argument against animal communication.  
D. Paragraphs 1 and 5 both give details only about whales.

**答案（维护信息）：** A  
**考点：** 逐段辨功能，识别开头总述与结尾收束均可承担概括作用。  
**解析：** 第一段先提出动物交流并概括声音、气味和肢体语言三种方式；中间三段依次展开；第五段回到人类与动物交流的总体话题，收束全文。因此，概括作用不只出现在开头，结尾也能回扣中心。

**来源与核验：** 广东省珠海市紫荆中学桃园校区中考一模英语试题，阅读理解原题第361—365题基础上的结构功能改编。按五段内容核验；与01/04、02/01及10单元文章复用，训练目标不同。 原文将 amoeba 列为 animal，改为 organisms；变形虫属于 Amoebozoa 原生生物，分类依据：[OpenStax Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/23-3-groups-of-protists)。

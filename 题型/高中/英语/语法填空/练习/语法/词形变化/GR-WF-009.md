## 题目

填写括号内所给词的适当形式：What we have had is just part of the truth. We should try to get \_\_\_\_ information about it. (far)

## 答案

- 1: further

## 解析

语境表示需要“进一步的”信息，further information 为常用表达。

## 判分说明

只接受 further；不接受 farther。

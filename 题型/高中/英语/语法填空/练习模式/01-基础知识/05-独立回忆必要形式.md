### 1

按顺序写出throw的过去式和过去分词。

Soon after moving to Switzerland, I ________ (throw) a house-warming party and was greatly surprised when all 30 guests showed up exactly on time.

- 答案：threw；thrown
- 考点：形式回忆
- 解析：throw的过去式是threw，过去分词是thrown，两者不同。

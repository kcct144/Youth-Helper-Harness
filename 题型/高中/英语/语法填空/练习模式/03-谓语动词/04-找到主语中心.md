# 找到主语中心

### 1

And in the process, I’ve experienced things that really surprise me at times. The “sunshine scent (香味)” of freshly sunned clothes ________ (be) one of them.

- 答案：is
- 考点：主语中心；一般现在时一致
- 解析：主语中心是单数scent，of freshly sunned clothes修饰scent；不能让复数clothes决定be的形式。因此填is。

### 2

Further, the Silk Route Garden around the greenhouse ________ (walk) visitors through a journey influenced by the ancient Silk Road, by which silk as well as many plant species came to Britain for the first time.

- 答案：walks
- 考点：主语中心；分句边界；第三人称单数
- 解析：主语中心是单数the Silk Route Garden，around the greenhouse是修饰语。walk在这里表示带领游览，按本关规定的一般现在时填walks。后面的came属于另一个从句。

### 3

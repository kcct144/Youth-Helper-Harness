### 1

Most travel agents are willing ________ (tailor) travel arrangements to meet individual requirements.

- 答案：to tailor
- 考点：不定式；be willing to do
- 解析：be willing to do表示愿意做某事，这里是愿意按个人需要定制旅行安排，填to tailor。

### 2

Many old men are opposed to ________ (cut) down the 100-year-old tree for rebuilding a shopping center.

- 答案：cutting
- 考点：动名词；介词to
- 解析：be opposed to中的to是介词，后接cutting，表示反对砍树。不能因为看见to就填写动词原形。
- 来源：专题04 语法填空之动名词100题（原卷版）.docx，原第42题，正文段落51；配套解析版同题号。

### 3

They didn’t know the native language, so they couldn’t make themselves ________ (understand).

- 答案：understood
- 考点：过去分词作补足语
- 解析：themselves是被别人理解的对象，make themselves understood表示让别人明白他们的意思，过去分词作宾语补足语。

### 4

________ (bury) herself in painting, the little girl was fully immersed in the wonderful world of colors.

- 答案：Burying / Having buried
- 考点：现在分词作状语；逻辑主语
- 解析：bury oneself in表示专心于；题干保留herself，女孩主动投入画画，使用Burying herself in painting作状语。若强调先投入画画、随后沉浸其中，也可用Having buried；句首大写。

### 6

### 7

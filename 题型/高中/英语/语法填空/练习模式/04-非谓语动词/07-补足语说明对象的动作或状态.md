# 补足语说明对象的动作或状态

## 做前说明

```text
补足语补充说明一个对象的动作或状态。本关练rely on someone to do（依靠某人做）、be encouraged to do（被鼓励做）、make oneself understood（让自己的意思被理解）和have someone scared（使某人害怕）。
先看具体搭配，再判断对象是行动者还是承受者，写全形式。
```

### 1

My host family are big fans of country music, so I can always rely on them ________ (introduce) me to some great songs.

- 答案：to introduce
- 考点：不定式补足语
- 解析：rely on them to introduce表示依靠他们推荐歌曲。

### 2

They didn’t know the native language, so they couldn’t make themselves ________ (understand).

- 答案：understood
- 考点：过去分词补足语
- 解析：themselves是被理解的对象，用过去分词understood。

### 3

Experiments show that when kids are encouraged ________ (share) what they have, they’re roughly twice as likely to be generous later.

- 答案：to share
- 考点：不定式补足语

### 4

When Nick runs ahead, I hear him barking excitedly. I sometimes call him back, worried that he might have someone ________ (scare).

- 答案：scared
- 考点：过去分词补足语
- 解析：someone是被吓到的人；have someone scared描述使某人处于害怕的状态，用scared。

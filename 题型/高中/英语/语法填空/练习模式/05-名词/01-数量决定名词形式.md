### 1

The goal was two-fold: to celebrate cultural diversity as well as to promote the equal use of all six ________ (tongue).

- 答案：tongues
- 考点：名词复数
- 解析：six表示六种语言，tongue加s。

### 2

The system developed into different forms, leading to many ________ (variety) of dialects and characters.

- 答案：varieties
- 考点：名词复数拼写
- 解析：many表示多种，variety变y为i再加es。

### 3

There are many reasons, but one of the main ________ (factor) has been the Chinese writing system.

- 答案：factors
- 考点：名词复数
- 解析：one of后用复数名词表示多个因素中的一个，填factors。

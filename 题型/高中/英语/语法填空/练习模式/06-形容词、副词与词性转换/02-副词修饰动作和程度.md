# 副词修饰动作和程度

## 做前说明

```text
副词可以说明动作怎样发生，也可以说明形容词的程度。

```

### 1

Sam walked her to a nearby convenience store so that she could ________ (safe) wait for the police to take her home.

- 答案：safely
- 考点：副词修饰动词
- 解析：修饰wait，safe变为safely，保留e。

### 2

Landing on the moon’s far side is ________ (extreme) challenging.

- 答案：extremely
- 考点：副词修饰形容词
- 解析：修饰challenging的程度，使用extremely。

### 3

When he looked down, he ________ (accidental ) slipped and fell over the edge.

- 答案：accidentally
- 考点：副词修饰动作
- 解析：说明slipped这个动作意外发生，用副词accidentally。
- 来源：专题11 语法填空之形容词副词100题（原卷版）.docx，原第17题，正文段落21。原汇编标签未官方核源。

### 4

As the small boat moved ________ (gentle) along the river he was left speechless by the mountains being silently reflected in the water.

- 答案：gently
- 考点：副词拼写
- 解析：空格说明船移动的方式，gentle变为gently。

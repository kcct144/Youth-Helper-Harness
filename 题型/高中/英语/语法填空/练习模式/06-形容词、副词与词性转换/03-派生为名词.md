### 1

Tanya’s shop offers fashion clothes for women to rent rather than purchase them outright, providing a less expensive ________ (solve) to one-time event dressing.

- 答案：solution
- 考点：动词转名词
- 解析：a less expensive后需要单数名词，solve变为solution。

### 2

Since June 2017, right before the ________ (arrive) of the two new pandas, Meng Meng and Jiao Qing, I have been helping the panda keepers at the zoo to feel more comfortable and confident speaking English.

- 答案：arrival
- 考点：动词转名词
- 解析：the arrival of表示……的到来，这里需要名词arrival。

### 3

Cao and Wu also collected garbage along the road, in order to promote environmental ________ (protect).

- 答案：protection
- 考点：派生名词
- 解析：environmental修饰中心名词，protect派生为protection；这里指环境保护这一活动，不加复数。

### 4

We gained ________ (admit) to the conference by showing our press passes.

- 答案：admission
- 考点：派生名词
- 解析：gain admission to表示获准进入，admit派生为名词admission；此处表示进入资格，不加复数。

### 1

“In a Go game, each move should serve a long-term goal. You try to lead the opponent into your trap and force them to follow your ________ (guide) till they lose,” explains Wang Wei, a Go player among the visitors to the exhibition.

- 答案：guidance
- 考点：动词转名词
- 解析：follow的宾语由your限定，填不可数名词guidance。

### 2

### 3

- 答案：increasingly
- 考点：副词修饰形容词

### 4

### 1

A company ________ (represent) wrote back and told me the airline was switching over from plastic to paper cups on all of its 1,200 daily flights.

- 答案：representative
- 考点：派生名词与单数
- 解析：a company representative表示一位公司代表，使用单数名词。

### 2

________ (represent) from 40 countries were present at the climate summit in Stockholm, Sweden.

- 答案：Representatives
- 考点：派生名词与复数
- 解析：来自40个国家的代表们作主语，were也对应复数，用Representatives，句首大写。
- 来源：专题07 语法填空之名词100题（原卷版）.docx，原第44题，正文段落51。

### 3

The themes of traditional fables often deal with simple truths about everyday life. However, Carson’s theme is a more weighty ________ (warn) about environmental destruction.

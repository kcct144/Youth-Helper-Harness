### 1

Small, rocky planets are the ________ (hard) of all to detect, but they are also the ones astrobiologists (天体生物学家) would most like to find.

- 答案：hardest
- 考点：最高级
- 解析：of all表明在所有相关对象中比较，填hardest。

### 3

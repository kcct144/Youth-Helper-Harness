# 名词前的物主形式

## 做前说明

```text
名词前的物主形式表示谁的东西或性质。先找指代对象，再看后面有没有名词。
本关的it指事物，后面都还保留名词，因此用its；its不带表示省略的撇号。
```

### 1

This area, with ________ (it) unique and breathtaking natural beauty, must be well preserved for all people of the nation to enjoy - as a national park.

- 答案：its
- 考点：物主形式
- 解析：指This area的natural beauty，用its。

### 3

That’s why each Spring Festival, hundreds of millions of people travel home to reunite (团聚) with ________ (they) relatives.

- 答案：their
- 考点：物主限定形式
- 解析：指hundreds of millions of people的亲属，they在名词relatives前变为their。

### 4

In the Digital Age, we’re used to having what we need immediately and right at ________ (we) fingertips.

# 动作回到自己

## 做前说明

```text
当对象与句子主语指同一个人或同一群人，常需反身代词表达自己。
```

### 1

Over time, I’ve found ________ (I) feeling extremely at home here. And in the process, I’ve experienced things that really surprise me at times.

- 答案：myself
- 考点：反身代词
- 解析：I发现的是自己在这里感到自在，使用myself。

### 3

Mary is an easy-going girl. Wherever she goes, she can adjust ________ (she) to new conditions soon.

- 答案：herself
- 考点：反身代词
- 解析：Mary使自己适应新环境，adjust oneself to...中的反身代词与she对应，填herself。

### 1

Over the last two years, some supermarkets have started selling chicken or salad in packs designed with two halves containing separate portions (份). Then, when you use one section, ________ other stays fresh.

- 答案：the
- 考点：两者范围
- 解析：题干已说明两部分，用完其中一个，the other指剩下的另一个。
- 来源：专题09 语法填空之冠词100题（原卷版）.docx，原第5题，正文段落8。（2024年浙江 1月卷）

### 2

Everybody wears ________ same style of clothes. Uniforms can be useful in unexpected ways. A school in Ireland has introduced an interesting new uniform.

- 答案：the
- 考点：固定表达
- 解析：the same style表示同一种款式，same前用the。

### 3

Go is one of ________ earliest binary-based (基于二元的) games.

- 答案：the
- 考点：最高级前定冠词
- 解析：one of the earliest...表示最早的一批游戏之一，最高级前填the。

### 4

Kangaroos and koalas are both native to Australia. The former is quite vigorous, while ________ latter looks lazy.

- 答案：the
- 考点：已知对象
- 解析：the former与the latter指前面已经提出的两者，空格填the。

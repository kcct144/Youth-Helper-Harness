### 1

The price of fashion – economically and environmentally – has led to the rise of ________ new way of dressing, and it’s beginning to take off in Australia, too.

- 答案：a
- 考点：泛指
- 解析：引入一种新的穿衣方式，new way是泛指单数名词短语，填a。
- 来源：专题09 语法填空之冠词100题（原卷版）.docx，原第1题，正文段落4。（2025年浙江 1月卷）

# 搭配中的介词

## 做前说明

```text
介词常是短语的一部分，需要连同意义一起记。
本关练focus on/upon（集中注意于）、keep an eye on（照看）、ban...from doing（禁止做）和recover from（从……恢复）。不要只背孤立介词。
```

## 题目

### 1

Customers today look past the fact that something is secondhand and focus instead ________ the fact that they have something unique to wear and are not overstuffing their own wardrobes (衣柜) or contributing to landfill.

- 答案：on / upon
- 考点：介词搭配
- 解析：focus on/upon the fact表示把注意力放在这一事实上；两种都接受。
- 来源：专题10 语法填空之介词100题（原卷版）.docx，原第2题，正文段落5。 (2025浙江1月卷)

### 2

I never worried about my son while I was away because my mother kept an eye ________ him.

- 答案：on
- 考点：介词搭配
- 解析：keep an eye on him表示照看他。

### 3

He has been banned ________ taking part in games for half a year, which has a bad effect on his career.

- 答案：from
- 考点：动词搭配
- 解析：ban someone from doing...表示禁止某人做某事，被动结构保留from。

### 4

I hope you can take at least one hour’s exercise each day after you recover ________ your illness.

- 答案：from
- 考点：动词搭配
- 解析：recover from an illness表示从疾病中恢复，填from。

# 时间与起点关系

## 做前说明

```text
介词表达时间、地点等关系。本关练from...to...的起终点、in the evening、in/during某个历史时期，以及not...until/till...表示直到某时才发生。
先理解当前关系，再填词。
```

### 1

Now, Cao has started the second part of his dream to walk along the Belt and Road route. He flew 4,700 kilometers ________ Xi’an to Kashgar, planning to hike back to Xi’an in five months.

- 答案：from
- 考点：起终点
- 解析：行程从西安到喀什，使用from Xi’an to Kashgar。

### 2

Helen was walking down the street late ________ the evening, her arms filled with grocery bags.

- 答案：in
- 考点：时间表达
- 解析：in the evening表示在晚上，late进一步说明时间较晚。

### 3

The Xi'an City Wall is the most complete city wall that has survived China's long history. It was built originally to protect the city ________ the Tang dynasty and has now been completely restored (修复).

- 答案：in / during
- 考点：时间关系
- 解析：表示在唐朝这一历史时期，in或during均可，不能因参考答案只列in排除during。

### 4

Ecotourism has its origin with the environmental movement of the 1970s. It was not widely accepted as a travel concept ________ the late 1980s.

- 答案：until / till
- 考点：时间关系

# 所属关系与介词后的关系词

## 做前说明

```text
whose可以表示从句中的名词属于谁；介词放在关系词前时，要同时看先行词和介词需要的对象。
本关分别练人的所属关系，以及从事情中学习的表达。
```

### 1

The color she chose came in a box which had a picture of a woman ________ hair color looked just perfect.

- 答案：whose
- 考点：所属关系
- 解析：hair color属于a woman，填whose限定hair color。

### 2

William Hastie once suggested that history informs us of past mistakes from ________ we can learn without repeating them.

- 答案：which
- 考点：介词加关系词
- 解析：from的对象是past mistakes，使用from which。
- 来源：专题13 语法填空之定语从句100题（原卷版）.docx，原第20题，正文段落25。（2021天津卷）

### 3

In ancient China lived an artist, ________ paintings were almost lifelike. The artist’s reputation had made him proud.

- 答案：whose
- 考点：所属关系
- 解析：paintings属于an artist，用whose修饰paintings，不用who直接修饰名词。

### 4

Many lessons are now available online, from ________ students can choose for free.

- 答案：which

### 6

- 答案：which
- 考点：介词后的关系代词

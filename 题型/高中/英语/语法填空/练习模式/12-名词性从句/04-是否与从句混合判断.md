### 1

It is still under discussion ________ the messy old bus station should be replaced with a modern hotel or not.

- 答案：whether
- 考点：whether引导主语从句
- 解析：主语从句后置在形式it之后，whether...or not说明是否更换旧车站。
- 来源：专题14 语法填空之名词性从句100题（原卷版）.docx，原第30题，正文段落36。

### 2

Oliver says if you're lucky enough to have someone close to you who enjoys cooking, ask them ________ you can join in when it's possible.

- 答案：if / whether
- 考点：是否宾语从句
- 解析：ask后询问是否可以加入做饭，用if或whether。
- 来源：专题14 语法填空之名词性从句100题（原卷版）.docx，原第16题，正文段落21。（2020年北京卷）

### 3

We are delighted at the news ________ our daughter won the first prize in the English speech contest.

- 答案：that
- 考点：从句混合
- 解析：news后的从句陈述获奖消息，填that，不表示是否。

### 4

When shall we discuss the problem ________ we should continue to invest money in this project or not?

- 答案：whether
- 考点：同位语从句表示是否
- 解析：problem后的从句说明要讨论的问题，whether...or not表示是否，不能用if代替这里的whether。

### 5

According to the theory, spending 20 minutes in a park daily helps to reduce stress, regardless of ________ people exercise in it or simply take a walk.

- 答案：whether
- 考点：介词后的是否从句
- 解析：regardless of后接whether从句，or连接两种可能，此处不用if。

### 6

- 考点：主语从句

### 7

Everyone on the bus began talking about ________ the boy had done, and the crowd of strangers suddenly became friendly to one another.

- 答案：what
- 考点：宾语从句
- 解析：about后的从句整体作介词宾语，what在从句中作had done的宾语，表示男孩所做的事。

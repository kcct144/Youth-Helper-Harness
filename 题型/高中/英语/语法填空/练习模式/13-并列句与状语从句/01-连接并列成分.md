### 1

It’s been an honor to watch the panda programme develop ________ to see the pandas settle into their new home.

- 答案：and
- 考点：并列不定式短语
- 解析：to watch...和to see...并列，填and。

### 2

The “First International Tea Day Tea Road Cooperative Initiative” issued (发布) at the ceremony calls for people working in the tea industry to come together to promote international cooperation ________ cultural exchanges.

### 3

Tu says that the balance between the black and white pieces, the beauty in the strategic placement of the pieces, ________ the energy flow following each move inspired artists to create oil paintings, sculptures, digitally generated pictures and silk-screen prints for the exhibition.

- 答案：and
- 考点：连接并列名词短语
- 解析：balance、beauty与energy flow共同作inspired的主语，最后一项前用and。

### 4

I found the contact information of the company ________ emailed its president.

- 答案：and
- 考点：连接并列谓语
- 解析：found与emailed是I先后完成的两个动作，用and并列连接。

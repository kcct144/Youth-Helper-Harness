### 1

But for all its ancient buildings, Beijing is also a place ________ welcomes the fast-paced development of modern life, with 21st-century architectural wonders standing side by side with historical buildings of the past.

- 答案：that / which
- 考点：定语从句
- 解析：从句修饰place，并缺少welcomes的主语，填that或which。
- 来源：专题13 语法填空之定语从句100题（原卷版）.docx，原第12题，正文段落16。（2023全国乙卷）

### 2

We are delighted at the news ________ our daughter won the first prize in the English speech contest.

- 答案：that
- 考点：同位语从句
- 解析：从句说明news的内容，本身结构完整，填that。
- 来源：专题14 语法填空之名词性从句100题（原卷版）.docx，原第25题，正文段落31。

### 3

- 答案：so
- 考点：结果状语从句
- 解析：这里的空在主句，sharp与that从句组成so...that结果结构。

### 7

Everyone on the bus began talking about ________ the boy had done, and the crowd of strangers suddenly became friendly to one another.

- 答案：what
- 考点：宾语从句
- 解析：about后的从句整体作介词宾语，what在从句中作had done的宾语，表示男孩所做的事。

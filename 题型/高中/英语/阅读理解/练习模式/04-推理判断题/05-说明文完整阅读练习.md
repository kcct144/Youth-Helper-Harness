## 题目

### 阅读材料

① In the Taishan Mountain scenic area in Tai’an, Shandong province, a robotic dog was tested. Its main job was to carry heavy loads, such as garbage, acting as a “porter” (脚夫) on the mountain and reducing the need for human workers.

② Every year, Taishan Mountain receives millions of visitors. These tourists produce nearly 24,000 tons of waste every year. The terrain (地形) in the mountain area makes it harder and more costly to clean and transport the garbage, as large machines cannot be used and the garbage has to be collected by hand.

③ To solve this problem, the scenic area tested robotic dogs for waste collection. The tested robotic dogs were some of the fastest and most advanced industrial-grade quadruped (四足的) robots on the market. They could cross obstacles (障碍) easily, keep good balance, and carry heavy loads for a long time.

④ Tests were carried out over three days at the base, middle and top of the mountain. The robotic dogs, equipped with a shelf and cargo (货物) box that could carry 30 to 40 kilograms, were tested for climbing ability and load-bearing capacity on the challenging terrain.

⑤ Results showed that they could reach the top of the mountain from the base within two hours, while the average time for adult tourists not carrying a heavy load was around four hours. The robots could operate for four to six hours on a full charge. During the tests, the robotic dogs could move across more than 80 percent of the mountain’s terrain.

⑥ Many tourists were attracted by these robotic dogs. Wang Yang, a tourist from Heilongjiang Province, said, “I saw it climbing and took a photo with it. It’s truly amazing.”

### 题目

1.（原题316）What is the main job of the robotic dog in the Taishan Mountain scenic area?

- A: To guide tourists.
- B: To carry heavy things like rubbish.
- C: To clean the scenic area directly.
- D: To protect the environment.

**答案（维护信息）：** B  
**考点：** 查找材料直接说明的主要用途。  
**解析：** 第一段明确说机器人主要搬运垃圾等重物，像山上的“搬运工”。

2.（原题317）Why is it difficult to clean and transport the garbage in Taishan Mountain?

- A: Because there are too many tourists.
- B: Because the garbage is too heavy.
- C: Because cleaners can’t use big machines to help them do cleaning.
- D: Because the tourists don’t cooperate with the cleaners.

**答案（维护信息）：** C  
**考点：** 把原因与文中条件对应。  
**解析：** 第二段说山地地形使大型机器无法使用，垃圾只能靠手工收集，因此清运更困难、成本更高。

3.（原题318，选项修订）Which detail about the robotic dogs is NOT mentioned in the passage?

- A: What they mainly carry.
- B: How long the tests lasted.
- C: How long they could work on a full charge.
- D: Who made the robotic dogs.

**答案（维护信息）：** D  
**考点：** 检查所问细节是否在文中出现。  
**解析：** 文中说明了机器狗搬运什么、测试持续多久、满电可运行多久，没有说明制造商是谁。原题选项中的“外观”和“颜色”在文中都未提及，可能造成两个正确选项，故改写为唯一未提及的“制造商”。

4.（原题319）What can be inferred from Paragraph 5?

- A: Robotic dogs are more suitable for long-distance mountain climbing.
- B: Robotic dogs can replace all human workers in mountain-related work.
- C: With a full charge, robotic dogs can make at least two round trips on the mountain.
- D: The robotic dogs may greatly improve the efficiency of garbage collection.

**答案（维护信息）：** D  
**考点：** 根据测试结果作有限推断。  
**解析：** 机器狗能载重并在较短时间到达山顶，满电可连续运行数小时，说明它们有望提升山地垃圾清运效率。文中没有说能替代全部工人或完成两次往返。

5.（原题320）In which part of a newspaper can we probably find this passage?

- A: Environment.
- B: Technology.
- C: Tourism.
- D: Entertainment.

**答案（维护信息）：** B  
**考点：** 根据文章核心对象判断可能的栏目。  
**解析：** 全文介绍机器狗的功能、测试和运行表现，核心是机器人技术，对应 Technology。

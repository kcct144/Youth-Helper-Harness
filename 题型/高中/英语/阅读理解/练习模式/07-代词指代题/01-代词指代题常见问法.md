# 01 代词指代题常见问法

## 做前说明

```
代词指代题问某个代词具体指什么，常见标志：

- What does "it" in Paragraph 3 refer to?
- What does the underlined word "they" refer to?

常见代词：it / they / them / this / that / these / those / one / ones。

本关练识别考点：面对一篇阅读原题和它的几道小题，找出哪道在考代词指代。先看题干要判断什么，不用先做完全部小题；常见问法帮助识别，最终以完整题意为准。
```

## 题目

### 阅读材料

Have you ever watched amazing drone (无人机) light shows? They’re really cool to watch! These shows often happen during big events like the Olympics or national celebrations. People really love **them**.

You might be wondering how these shows work. Well, they use a lot of drones with bright lights on them. First, someone programs a computer to tell the drones what to do. Then, that program goes to each drone. After that, people on the ground use the computer to make the drones move. When everything is ready, the show starts. All the drones fly up at the same time and move in the sky in a beautiful way.

The number of drones can make different effects. For example, some shapes need more drones than others. Simple shapes need fewer drones, and more complicated shapes need more. The number of the viewers and how far away they are also decide how many drones are needed so everyone can see the show well.

Even though these drone light shows are really cool, there are still ways to make them better. New tools and technology can help make the shows even more amazing. Also, the people who design the shows are trying to find new ways to make them even better.

原卷题干：

- 原题26：What does the underlined word “them” refer to?
- 原题27：What is paragraph 2 mainly about?
- 原题28：What decides the number of drones in a show?
- 原题29：According to the last paragraph, what can we expect in the future?
- 原题30：Which is the best title of the passage?

### 题目

哪一道原卷题直接询问代词所指的对象？

A. 原题26  
B. 原题27  
C. 原题29  
D. 原题30

**答案（维护信息）：** A  
**考点：** 代词指代题问法识别。  
**解析：** 原题26用 “What does … refer to?” 询问 them 的指代对象。原题27问段落大意，29问未来推断，30问全文标题。

**来源与核验：** 2025年广东省梅州市兴宁市实验学校、宁江中学中考二模英语试题，阅读理解B篇，原题第26—30题；此处只判断设问类别。

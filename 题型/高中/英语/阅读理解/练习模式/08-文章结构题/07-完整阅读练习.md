## 题目

### 阅读材料

① A popular saying goes, “One person’s junk is another person’s treasure.” There certainly is some truth in it. Rather than using paint, ink or other traditional materials, many artists are turning to discarded (废弃的) materials to create their artwork.

② It’s a well-known fact that artists have been using discarded resources for a long time. One of the reasons for this is that traditional art resources such as paint, clay and metal can be extremely expensive. Another reason is that some artists want to bring attention to the fact that people throw away a lot of perfectly useful items.

③ Artists may seem avant-garde (前卫的) by using garbage as the material for their artwork. For example, most people wouldn’t think that a plastic bag would have an artistic purpose. However, some artists have shown photos of breathtaking landscapes and ocean surfaces created using only plastic bags. And something called “auto-destructive art” is another example. The idea has existed since the 1960s. This means that an object doesn’t start out as art. It becomes beautiful as it breaks or becomes old and worn. If you have seen broken glass, you can understand this idea better.

④ Some artists just want to create something that will make people think about important problems. One popular theme in art is the importance of reducing environmental waste. A few years ago, a large work of art called *World of Litter* received international attention. The artist, Peter Singer, created a huge globe that floated on water. It was bigger than some of the local boats! Singer created it by collecting hundreds of bottles and other forms of plastic waste.

⑤ The discarded materials make beautiful artwork. Besides, they provide hope for the future by bringing about environmental awareness.

### 题目

1.（原题 46）Why do artists use discarded materials to create art according to Paragraph 2?

- A: All traditional art materials cost too much.
- B: They want to sell their art at higher prices.
- C: Traditional art materials are hard to get.
- D: They want to remind people not to waste useful things.

**答案（维护信息）：** D  
**考点：** 查找段落中的原因。  
**解析：** 第二段说，一些艺术家希望人们注意到大家会丢弃仍然有用的物品，对应 D。传统材料贵是其中一个原因，但 A 的 “all” 过度概括。

2.（原题 47）Which of the following is an example of “auto-destructive art”?

- A: An old famous painting that is kept in a museum.
- B: A bottle filled with beans on a new table.
- C: A broken bicycle in a yard with plants growing around it.
- D: A picture of a tree.

**答案（维护信息）：** C  
**考点：** 把定义应用到新例子。  
**解析：** 文中解释 auto-destructive art：物品起初不是艺术品，而是在破损或变旧的过程中变得美。破损的自行车及周围植物最符合这一描述。

3.（原题 48）According to the passage, which of the following may be one of the reasons that Peter Singer created *World of Litter*?

- A: To create a tool for water transportation.
- B: To collect plastic bottles.
- C: To raise awareness about environmental waste.
- D: To make himself become famous.

**答案（维护信息）：** C  
**考点：** 结合段落目的推断具体作品的用意。  
**解析：** 第四段先说艺术家想让人思考重要问题，并指出减少环境垃圾是常见主题，随后举 *World of Litter* 为例，因此作品可能旨在提高人们对环境垃圾的关注。

4.（原题 49）Which of the following best describes the structure of the passage?

- A: ①②→③④→⑤
- B: ①→②③④→⑤
- C: ①→②③→④⑤
- D: ①②③→④→⑤

**答案（维护信息）：** B  
**考点：** 识别总述、展开和总结的段落关系。  
**解析：** 第①段提出艺术家使用废弃材料创作的现象；第②—④段分别说明原因、创作方式和实例；第⑤段总结其艺术与环保意义。对应 B。

5.（原题 50）What is the purpose of this passage?

- A: To inform about discarded materials being used for art.
- B: To share the idea that garbage makes better art.
- C: To call on people to fight against pollution.
- D: To teach the readers to make art out of garbage.

**答案（维护信息）：** A  
**考点：** 判断全文主要说明内容与写作目的。  
**解析：** 文章介绍艺术家如何使用废弃材料创作、这样做的原因及例子，主要是告知这一现象；没有教制作步骤，也没有单纯号召读者。

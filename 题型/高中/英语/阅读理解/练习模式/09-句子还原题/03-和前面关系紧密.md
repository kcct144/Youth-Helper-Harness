## 题目

### 阅读材料

In a glass pool, some fish were swimming happily. When the visitors took a closer look, they found these colorful fish were actually robots! This amazing scene happened at the World Digital Education Exhibition in Wuhan. Scientists from Wuhan University spent much time developing these red, yellow and blue robotic fish.

The fish are about 53 centimeters long. The joints (关节) in their heads and tails are designed in a special way, allowing them to swim smoothly and naturally. ________ This is the fruit of the latest development of bionic engineering (仿生工程). It cleverly mixes nature and technology to create new inventions.

James Crabbe, a scientist at the University of Oxford, explained the bionic fish could help researchers learn more about how fish behave. Take fish swarming for an example: it is a way for fish to swim in large groups to stay safe. In this way, they can also save energy and find food more easily. Studying fish swarming helps scientists understand how fish live and how the environment influences them.

To scare away dangerous animals, the bionic fish show different colors with the lights built into their bodies. Inside each fish, there is a sender to give out signals (信号). When meeting things, the signals are sent back quickly and caught by the receiver in the body. In this way, the bionic fish can “see” and stay away from things in their way. Now they are being used to study the Yangtze River’s water quality and play a key role in protecting our living environment.

### 题目

第2段空格最适合填入哪句话？

A. They act almost like real ones.  
B. Scientists are interested in them.  
C. It is not difficult to make them.  
D. Visitors can easily buy them now.

**答案（维护信息）：** A  
**考点：** 句子还原；前一句提供的动作和状态线索。  
**解析：** 前句说机器鱼在特殊关节设计下可以游得流畅、自然，A 接着概括它们的表现几乎像真的鱼。后句再把这种仿生效果联系到仿生工程。其他选项不能承接“游得像真的”这一具体信息。

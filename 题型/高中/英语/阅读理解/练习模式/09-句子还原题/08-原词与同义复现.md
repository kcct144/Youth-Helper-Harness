# 08 原词与同义复现

## 做前说明

```

看看候选句里的词和哪一处"接得上"：出现了同一个词、同一个意思的不同说法，或者同一个话题更细或更粗的说法，那里往往就是它的位置。
```

## 题目

### 阅读材料

You can put a stamp on it, you can write an address on it, and you can send it through the mail. No, it’s not a package (包裹). It’s the amazing coconut (椰子)! The coconut is different from most other fruits because every part of it is useful. People have been using coconuts in different ways. Here are some of them.

One exciting use of the coconut is as a kind of energy. Scientists are working on using coconuts for energy because they are cheap and clean. Coconut oil has already been made into fuel (燃料). It can propel cars, trucks, ships and many more.

Students at Baylor University found that coconut husks (外壳) can take in water very well. So mixing coconut husks with dry soil helps the soil to hold onto more of the water. The students also found that coconut husks can take the place of plastic in some cases. If this is possible, it will help people reduce the use of plastic.

▲ That’s why some countries allow people to send the coconut without any packaging. Each year, tourists post about 3,000 of these “coconut postcards”.

Coconuts grow naturally in over 80 countries of the world. They require only sun and proper rain. No special care is needed. Every year, more than 61 million tons of coconuts are produced. It is not surprising, then, that people want to find as many uses as possible for the coconut.

### 题目

第4段空格最适合填入哪句话？

A. Coconuts are popular in hot countries.  
B. Scientists are studying new ways to grow coconuts.  
C. Coconuts are used to make many kinds of drinks.  
D. The husk of the coconut is a perfect package.

**答案（维护信息）：** D  
**考点：** 句子还原；核对关键词 coconut、husk、package 的复现和对应。  
**解析：** 前一段重复提到 coconut husks，并说它们在某些情况下可以替代塑料；D 再次提到 coconut 的 husk，并把它与 package 联系起来，承接前文并解释后句不需额外包装寄送的原因。不能仅凭重复了同一个词作答，还要看意思是否相连。

**来源与核验：** 广东省汕头市龙湖区龙湖实验学校 2025—2026 学年上学期八年级英语科第二次学情反馈训练题，阅读理解原题第19题。与本单元第07关复用同一文章和小题，训练目标分别聚焦因果关系与词语复现；重复材料不作为未见材料迁移证据。

## 题目

### 阅读材料

① As winter 2025 comes, Shenzhen Bay Park and other coastal (沿海的) parks do a kind thing—they close early and turn off all lights at night. This action tries to make a quiet, dark place for migratory birds (候鸟) stopping here during their long trip.

② Shenzhen is a vital stop on migratory birds’ flying path between East Asia and Australia. Every year, from October to May, nearly 100,000 birds of over 250 species stop here. The city has done more to protect these “guests”. Workers put up special streetlights in parks—these lights have soft yellow light that doesn’t hurt birds, and they’re good for them to build nests (鸟巢). Many buildings near the wetlands also use special glass, so birds won’t hit it by accident.

③ ________ Workers have planted over 2,000 trees and fixed the natural water systems to make wetlands better. This gives birds plenty of food and safe places to live. Smart tools like AI cameras help watch birds without troubling them. Also, volunteers organize activities like cleaning the beach to keep the birds’ home tidy. These efforts show that a modern city can be a true friend to nature. Cities and nature can live well together when we care for wild animals. Everyone’s small actions can help protect the beautiful connection between humans and nature.

### 题目

1.（原题221）Why did Shenzhen’s coastal parks close early in winter?

- A: To save energy for the city.
- B: To create a quiet place for birds.
- C: To reduce noise in the area.
- D: To stop people watching birds.

**答案（维护信息）：** B  
**考点：** 查找行动的直接目的。  
**解析：** 第一段说公园提前关闭、夜间关灯，是为了给迁徙鸟类提供安静、黑暗的停歇地。

2.（原题222）What does the underlined word “vital” in Paragraph 2 mean?

- A: Important.
- B: Common.
- C: Quiet.
- D: Happy.

**答案（维护信息）：** A  
**考点：** 根据所在句判断词义。  
**解析：** 深圳是候鸟迁徙路线上的重要停歇点，vital 在此意为 important。

3.（原题223）Which of the following can be put in the blank in Paragraph 3?

- A: Improving wetlands is a big task.
- B: Special lights and glass help a lot.
- C: Birds need clean food and water.
- D: The protection is more than darkness.

**答案（维护信息）：** D  
**考点：** 根据后文扩展方向补入承上启下句。  
**解析：** 第二段已经介绍关灯、特殊路灯和玻璃等措施。空后转向修复湿地、种树、监测和志愿活动，说明鸟类保护不只是减少夜间光线，对应 D。

4.（原题224）How do workers watch the birds without troubling them?

- A: By using AI cameras.
- B: By using special glass.
- C: By standing behind trees.
- D: By looking through windows.

**答案（维护信息）：** A  
**考点：** 定位技术措施的具体用途。  
**解析：** 第三段明确说 AI cameras 帮助人们观察鸟类而不打扰它们。

5.（原题225）What is the best title for the passage?

- A: A Long Trip to Australia
- B: The Beautiful Coastal Parks
- C: A Warm Home for Birds
- D: The Changes of Shenzhen

**答案（维护信息）：** C  
**考点：** 用保护措施和总体目的概括全文。  
**解析：** 文章介绍深圳为迁徙鸟类提供安全栖息环境的多种措施，C概括这一主旨。

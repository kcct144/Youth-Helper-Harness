## Money Workshops

Want your children to manage money wisely? Bring them to our free workshops!

Our money workshops are for 7 to 12-year-old children. These workshops will be held in the city library on weekends. Each workshop can take up to 20 children.

### Workshop 1: Wise Spender

- Time: 9 a.m. — 10 a.m. Sat.
- Aims:
  - to understand “needs” and “wants” in spending
  - to learn how to make a budget plan
- Example Activity: Children are given some spending choices like buying a book or seeing a film. They work together and discuss what to spend on within a budget.

### Workshop 2: Smart Saver

- Time: 4 p.m. — 5 p.m. Sun.
- Aims:
  - to understand the benefits of saving
  - to learn how to open a bank account
- Example Activity: Children are divided into two groups. One group acts as bank managers and the other acts as customers who want to open an account.

### Why Choose Us?

We use real-world examples to help children learn to make smarter decisions on money.

### How To Book?

You need to complete a booking form and send it to us at booking@citylibrary.org. For more information, please visit www.citylibrary.org.

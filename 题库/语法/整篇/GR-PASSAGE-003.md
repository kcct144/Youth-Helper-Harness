## 题目

阅读下面材料，在空白处填入适当的内容（1个单词）或括号内单词的正确形式。

Jonny: Hey! I’m just practicing Tai Chi(太极). Would you like to join me?

Peter: I know nothing about it. Is it difficult?

Jonny: It seems easy, but you need a lot of practice. You just follow me like this.

Peter: OK. Don’t laugh （1）\_\_\_\_ me. I may look funny.

Jonny: Bend your knees slightly and reach out your arms like tree branches, naturally and （2）\_\_\_\_ (soft). Try to keep your body straight. Move slowly, then be sure to keep your balance and don’t let your body shake.

Peter: I cannot control my body well. My legs become （3）\_\_\_\_ (pain).

Jonny: Keep （4）\_\_\_\_ (hold) your position for a while. It helps develop your strength and flexibility. Raise your leg and let （5）\_\_\_\_ stay in the air for seconds.

Peter: I feel my legs shaking. I cannot do this any longer.

Jonny: Be patient! Tai Chi （6）\_\_\_\_ (call) “shadow boxing” in English. It asks you to act like water: to be flexible as well （7）\_\_\_\_ strong. In real competition, a Tai Chi master borrows the strength of the competitor and uses this energy to fight back. The （8）\_\_\_\_ (hard) you try to beat him, the more likely you will get hit. He controls you!

Peter: Unbelievable! Oh..., （9）\_\_\_\_ you don't mind, I'll stop and take a deep （10）\_\_\_\_ (breathe).

## 答案

- 1: at
- 2: softly
- 3: painful
- 4: holding
- 5: it
- 6: is called
- 7: as
- 8: harder
- 9: if
- 10: breath

## 解析

1. `laugh at sb.` 表示“嘲笑某人”。
2. 与 naturally 并列修饰动作 reach out，用副词 softly。
3. become 为系动词，后接形容词 painful 作表语。
4. `keep doing` 表示“继续做”，用 holding。
5. 指代前面的单数名词 leg，并作 let 的宾语，用 it。
6. Tai Chi 与 call 是被动关系，陈述一般事实，完整谓语为 is called。
7. `as well as` 构成“既……又……”的并列结构。
8. 与后面的 the more likely 构成“the + 比较级，the + 比较级”，用 harder。
9. “如果你不介意”是条件，用 if 引导条件状语从句。
10. `take a deep breath` 是固定搭配，用名词 breath。
